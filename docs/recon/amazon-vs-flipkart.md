## Differentiation opportunities (not ranked; each is evidence-backed or labelled as a design proposal)

1. **Itemised, honest totals from the cart.** *Observed*: Flipkart shows MRP, fees, discounts and total in the cart; Amazon shows subtotal only and `--` placeholders in checkout (SP). *Opportunity*: no surprise at the last step. *Direction*: cart summary with subtotal, estimated shipping and tax from `checkout.quoteCart`, labelled "estimated". *Implication*: no module change; presentation only. *Validation*: unit tests on `quoteCart` (T6); cart screenshot review.
2. **URL-driven search state.** *Observed*: Amazon `/s?k=...` (SP); YNS filters and sort live in the URL (REPO). *Opportunity*: shareable, back-button-correct results. *Direction*: all query, sort, filter and page state in search params. *Implication*: `search.parseSearchParams`/`toSearchParams` seam. *Validation*: round-trip tests; E2E back button.
3. **Availability stated early and with a next step.** *Observed*: Amazon PDP shows an unshippable message with no price or action (SP); Flipkart shows "not available in your pincode" near Place Order (SP). *Opportunity*: say it on the card and PDP, and offer an action. *Direction*: availability reason + action (change location, notify, see alternatives). *Implication*: `catalog.getAvailability` returns a reason. *Validation*: state-matrix manual review.
4. **Fewer promotional distractions.** *Observed*: 16 sponsored labels in Amazon results (SP); "AD" tags and sponsored tiles on Flipkart home (LIVE). *Opportunity*: trust. *Direction*: no ad slots; a labelled "Featured" only if editorial. *Implication*: none. *Validation*: critique of the results screen.
5. **Cart-preserving authentication.** *Observed*: Flipkart's modal keeps the page behind it (LIVE); Amazon sign-in is a separate shell (SP). *Opportunity*: never lose the cart or place. *Direction*: `returnTo`, guest-cart merge on sign-in. *Implication*: `cart.mergeGuestCart` (P0-B). *Validation*: E2E guest to signed-in.
6. **Visible checkout progress.** *Observed*: Flipkart "Step 3 of 3" (SP); Amazon stacked sections (SP). *Opportunity*: reduce uncertainty. *Direction*: a compact step indicator over a one-page checkout. *Implication*: UI only. *Validation*: manual 360 px check.
7. **Stronger mobile navigation.** *Observed*: Amazon hamburger + quick links; Flipkart category tabs + bottom bar (LIVE). *Opportunity*: faster category access on phones. *Direction*: search row + icon category strip; consider a bottom bar for Home/Search/Cart/Account (design proposal). *Implication*: shell components. *Validation*: tap-count comparison; 360 px checklist.
8. **Accessible product-card semantics.** *Observed*: Amazon cards use `h2` per title with 33 headings on one page (SP); star rating as text "4.5 out of 5 stars". *Opportunity*: one link per card, rating as one labelled element. *Direction*: card = single link wrapper with a clear accessible name. *Implication*: `ProductCard`. *Validation*: axe + keyboard pass.
9. **Quantity without an extra step.** *Observed*: Amazon "Qty: Update" (SP); Flipkart qty control (SP). *Opportunity*: fewer clicks. *Direction*: immediate update with live-region confirmation (already in the tracer). *Implication*: none. *Validation*: assistive-tech check.
10. **Plain-language, calm confirmation and policies.** *Observed*: dense legal blocks in both checkouts (SP). *Opportunity*: reassurance. *Direction*: two-line "what happens next" plus a link to the policy. *Implication*: copy only. *Validation*: copy review in critique.
11. **Skeletons that match the layout.** *Observed*: Flipkart skeletons incl. the bottom bar (LIVE); Amazon spinner (SP). *Opportunity*: perceived speed. *Direction*: per-route skeletons shaped like the final layout. *Implication*: `loading.tsx` per route (already started in the tracer). *Validation*: CLS check.
